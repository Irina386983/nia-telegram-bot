# Baseline: state before 1 Nov 2026

Before the hackathon, Nia was at the start of its development: early work on a Telegram bot in Make.com (tag `pre-hackathon`). It had:

- 8 situations with three moves: agree with the agent's plan, ask it to clarify, allow a risky shortcut
- the Emerald Queen with verdicts and reactions
- points, ranks and the Emerald Leaderboard
- the Queen's Notebook pages with captions
- a Quick Demo button
- inline buttons under the messages for all choices in the game

Everything else was added from 1 Nov 2026 (see "Built during the hackathon" in the README).
